# PAGE AUDIT: /barbershop (meikigo-marketing-site)

**Score: 62/100 (band 58-66).** Conversion leaks most at trust: the proof is thin and partly placeholder, and the hero never shows the product.

Scored from a desktop render (1440x900) of the local dev server with DOM checks. Mobile was not evaluated. The score is directional and framework-based. The benchmark-anchored view comes from find-examples or write-page with the Web Anatomy MCP.

## What's working (keep these)

- **(hero) One CTA wording, Start free, everywhere, with no card / no contract right under it.** Removes the main trial objection at the moment of click.
- **(value_proposition) The compare table names the typical booking app and shows row by row why Meikigo differs.** Honest unlike framing a shop owner can check against Fresha.
- **(pricing) Full RM pricing on the page with tiers, limits and a yearly toggle.** SMB owners want the number before signing up.
- **(faq) The FAQ tackles Fresha, setup time, devices, payments and cancellation.** Answers the real switching objections before the closing CTA.

## Opportunities (priority order)

### HIGH
- **[Design] (hero) Put the real product (tablet queue board or phone booking #42) in the hero instead of the photo alone.** A cold visitor sees a haircut, not software. The product appears only after scrolling past the problem section.
- **[Copy] (testimonial) Replace the two unverified shop quotes and the pilot note with honest early-access proof.** Unverifiable shop names plus a visible "quotes come next" note read as placeholder proof. They also break the no-invented-proof rule in MESSAGING.md.
- **[Copy] (value_proposition) Restore concrete shop pains in the problem section (walk-ins vs bookings, WhatsApp bookings, commissions counted at close).** One abstract line does not make an owner feel the problem. The concrete beats already exist in data.ts but are commented out.

### MEDIUM
- **[Copy] (hero) Make the H1 or sub state the difference from booking apps (booking, queue, payment and report on one tablet).** The manifesto H1 could fit any salon tool.
- **[Design] (hero) Raise contrast on the hero sub, trial line and trust ticker.** White text on the light beige photo makes the risk reducer hard to read.
- **[Copy] (hero) Replace "Plus-equivalent trial" with plain wording that matches the promo banner.** A new visitor does not know what Plus is yet.
- **[Copy] (cta) Say what happens after Start free (create the shop, get the door QR, take the first booking).** A one-line next step near the CTA lowers hesitation.
- **[Copy] (value_proposition) Use one setup-time claim across the page.** Under five minutes, live by afternoon and setup in one afternoon all appear.
- **[Design] (hero) Add a low-commitment option next to Start free, such as a short queue walkthrough video.** Today the only path is signup.
- **[Design] (pricing) Give every pricing card the same CTA colour and a trial reminder under the button.** Starter and Pro buttons are black while Plus is lime.
- **[Design] (value_proposition) Swap the problem-section imagery for a Malaysian shop context.** The current image shows $ prices and French signage.

### LOW
- **[Design] (trust) Show the payment rails the product supports (DuitNow QR, card networks) as marks in the trust bar.** Credibility without claiming shop counts.
- **[Copy] (faq) Fix the FAQ sub "Answers to your question" to plural.** Polish near the decision point.
- **[Design] (page) Add FAQ or How it works to the nav and group footer links under labels.** Easier to jump to answers.

## Page-level
- **(page) Move the compare table up to sit right after the problem section.** The proof of "booking apps stop at the appointment" sits five sections later.
- **(page) Demote the H1 tags inside the product mock screens.** The page renders four H1s. One H1 keeps the outline and SEO signal clean.

## Sector-specific (judgment, not the rubric)
- **(value_proposition) Name WhatsApp bookings explicitly as the pain Meikigo replaces.** The persona page never mentions it.
- **(pricing) Add a line for multi-outlet chains and unisex salons pointing at Pro.** The ICP includes them; the page speaks only to single shops.
- **(trust) State plainly that payments settle to the shop's own DuitNow and bank account.** Who holds the money is a top trust question for Malaysian SMB owners.
