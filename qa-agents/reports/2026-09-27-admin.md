# Meikigo exploratory test report — 2026-09-27

## Summary

This run covered only the **admin portal**, with three personas, all in WebKit. There were no crashes, JS exceptions, HTTP errors, blank screens or stuck loading. Login and navigation work.

The most important finding is on the Money → Payouts screens. The **"Net payout" column shows the gross amount** for every payout, while the Details panel shows a non-zero amount withheld. Finance approves irreversible payouts from this screen. The sidebar also needs two clicks to navigate after every page load. The remaining findings are accessibility issues and wording problems.

Two of the three goals were completed. The third persona gave up because the setting they were asked to change ("support hours closing at 7pm") does not exist. That outcome is correct, not a defect.

| App | Personas run | Goals completed | Confirmed bugs (by severity) | Usability issues |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Admin portal (`meiki-admin.test:3004`) | 3 | 2 of 3 | High 1 · Medium 1 · Low 3 (plus 1 unconfirmed Low) | 7 |

## Bugs

### BUG-001: The "Net payout" column shows the gross amount, not gross minus withheld

- **ID:** BUG-001
- **Severity:** High
- **Status:** CONFIRMED (seen in 2 sessions and reproduced by me)
- **App:** Admin portal
- **Page/route:** Money → Payouts, both the Queue (`/chip/settlements`) and History (`/chip/settlements?view=history`)

**Steps to reproduce**
1. Open http://meiki-admin.test:3004 and sign in with ADMIN_EMAIL / ADMIN_PASSWORD.
2. Click **Money** in the sidebar (click twice, see BUG-002).
3. On **Payouts → Queue**, click **Details** on the "Abu Barbershop" row.
4. Compare the **Net payout** column with the Details text.
5. Click **History** and open **Details** on "The Fade Room" (status Completed).

**Expected:** Net payout = Gross − Withheld. For example, RM 1,284.00 − RM 24.00 = **RM 1,260.00**, and RM 2,105.00 − RM 42.10 = **RM 2,062.90**.

**Actual:** On all 7 payouts, the Net payout column shows the gross amount.

| View | Shop | Net payout column | Details panel | Correct net |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Queue | Abu Barbershop | RM 1,284.00 | Gross 1,284.00 · Withheld 24.00 | RM 1,260.00 |
| Queue | Kedai Gunting Sri Muda | RM 475.00 | Gross 475.00 · Withheld 9.50 | RM 465.50 |
| Queue | The Fade Room | RM 88.00 | Gross 88.00 · Withheld 1.76 | RM 86.24 |
| History (Sending) | Kedai Gunting Sri Muda | RM 540.00 | Gross 540.00 · Withheld 9.00 | RM 531.00 |
| History (Completed) | The Fade Room | RM 2,105.00 | Gross 2,105.00 · Withheld 42.10 | RM 2,062.90 |
| History (Failed) | Kedai Gunting Sri Muda | RM 330.00 | Gross 330.00 · Withheld 6.60 | RM 323.40 |
| History (Rejected) | Abu Barbershop | RM 150.00 | Gross 150.00 · Withheld 3.00 | RM 147.00 |

The dashboard's "Needs you" list shows the same gross figures, for example "Abu Barbershop · payout RM 1,284.00".

**Related observations (please check while fixing):**
- **Settings → Billing → CHIP commission** says margin is "withheld at settlement". It lists every payment method (Card, DuitNow QR, FPX) as "No Meikigo margin", 0.00%. Yet every payout withholds about 2%. One or both of these screens is wrong.
- The withheld rate is not consistent. Most rows withhold exactly 2.00%, but Abu Barbershop withholds RM 24.00 on RM 1,284.00 (1.87%) and Kedai RM 9.00 on RM 540.00 (1.67%).

**Why High:** a Finance user gets a wrong money figure on the screen where they approve payouts. The page says "Approval … cannot be undone". The persona could not give her boss a reliable settled amount. All Money screens show a "Sample data … no money moves" banner, so no real money is affected yet. If the live API path uses the same field, this becomes **Critical**.

**Evidence**
- No oracle signal (this is a logic error, not a crash).
- Persona page snapshots: `admin-money-check__webkit/artifacts/page-2026-09-27T12-41-05-485Z.yml` (history, lines 72–101) and `admin-first-look__webkit/artifacts/page-2026-09-27T12-42-11-853Z.yml` (queue, lines 86–100).
- My screenshots: `_triage/repro-net-payout-history.png` and `_triage/repro-net-payout-queue.png`.
- Commission settings snapshot: `admin-change-setting__webkit/artifacts/page-2026-09-27T12-40-59-553Z.yml`.

**Found by:** admin-money-check__webkit (reported it in her diary), admin-first-look__webkit (same data in a snapshot)

### BUG-002: The first click on a sidebar link only expands the sidebar and does not navigate

- **ID:** BUG-002
- **Severity:** Medium
- **Status:** CONFIRMED (2 sessions, and I reproduced it twice)
- **App:** Admin portal
- **Page/route:** Left sidebar on every page (seen on `/`, `/support/open`, `/support/pii`, `/chip/subscription-invoices`)

**Steps to reproduce**
1. Sign in at http://meiki-admin.test:3004. After each page load the sidebar is a collapsed icon rail.
2. Click **Money (7)** in the sidebar.
3. Wait a few seconds.
4. Click **Money (7)** again.

**Expected:** One click opens the Money page.

**Actual:** The first click only expands the sidebar to full width, and the URL stays `/`. The second click navigates. The same thing happened when I clicked **Settings** from Monthly bills: after 30 seconds no navigation had happened, and a second click worked.

**Evidence**
- No oracle signal.
- Session logs show the URL unchanged after the first click:
  - `admin-first-look__webkit/artifacts/session-1790512782104/session.md`: Shop (lines 92–130), Customer (439–478), Money (609–634), Settings (747–772). Every sidebar link needed two clicks.
  - `admin-money-check__webkit/artifacts/session-1790512782104/session.md`: Money (lines 92–113). URL stayed `/`, and the persona then used the "Start a job" Money link instead.
- My screenshots: `_triage/repro-sidebar-first-click.png` (sidebar expanded, still on the Dashboard) and `_triage/repro-sidebar-settings-first-click.png` (sidebar expanded, still on Monthly bills).
- Diary: "The first click on a sidebar nav link … sometimes just expanded/highlighted the sidebar instead of navigating; I had to click a second time."

**Found by:** admin-first-look__webkit, admin-money-check__webkit

### BUG-003: The selected settings sub-tab has too little contrast and looks disabled

- **ID:** BUG-003
- **Severity:** Low
- **Status:** CONFIRMED (2 personas, and I reproduced it)
- **App:** Admin portal
- **Page/route:** Every Settings sub-page. The signal fired on `/settings/plan-caps`, `/settings/email-deliverability`, `/settings/subscription-billing`, `/settings/statutory-template`, `/settings/smtp`, `/settings/email-packages`, `/settings/chip-commission` and `/settings/addon-pricing`.

**Steps to reproduce**
1. Sign in and open **Settings → Billing → Plan caps**.
2. Look at the sub-tab row (Billing cycle · CHIP commission · **Plan caps** · Add-ons).

**Expected:** The current sub-tab is clearly highlighted and readable (text contrast of at least 4.5:1).

**Actual:** The selected sub-tab (`.button--secondary`) shows mid-grey text (`oklab(0.464 0 0)`) on `rgb(39,39,42)`, which is roughly **2.1:1**, in dark mode. The page you are on looks like the only disabled option.

**Evidence**
- Signal `a11y`, `color-contrast` (serious), sample `.button--secondary`. It appeared on 8 settings routes and was hit twice on `/settings/plan-caps`.
- My screenshot: `_triage/repro-plan-caps-contrast.png`.

**Found by:** admin-change-setting__webkit, admin-first-look__webkit

### BUG-004: The Money view switchers use `role="tablist"` but contain links, not tabs

- **ID:** BUG-004
- **Severity:** Low
- **Status:** CONFIRMED (reproduced by me on 3 pages)
- **App:** Admin portal
- **Page/route:** `/chip/settlements` (Queue/History), `/chip/disputes` (Queue/History), `/chip/subscription-invoices` (Outstanding/History)

**Steps to reproduce**
1. Open **Money → Payouts**.
2. Inspect the Queue/History switcher with a screen reader or an axe scan.

**Expected:** A `tablist` contains `role="tab"` children. Alternatively, since these are page links, use a plain `nav` instead of `tablist`.

**Actual:** `div[role="tablist"]` ("Payout view", "Payment complaint view", "Monthly bill view") contains two plain `<a>` elements and 0 tabs. Screen readers announce a tab list with no tabs.

**Evidence**
- Signal `a11y`, `aria-required-children` (**critical** impact per axe), `div[role="tablist"]` on `/chip/settlements?view=history`.
- My DOM check found the same structure on all three Money pages.

**Found by:** admin-first-look__webkit

### BUG-005: A React Aria "PressResponder without a pressable child" warning appears on every screen

- **ID:** BUG-005
- **Severity:** Low
- **Status:** CONFIRMED (3 sessions and 16 routes; also in my session)
- **App:** Admin portal
- **Page/route:** `/`, `/support/open`, `/support/pii`, `/chip/settlements`, `/chip/disputes`, and every `/settings/*` page

**Steps to reproduce**
1. Sign in and open any page with the browser console open.

**Expected:** No framework warnings.

**Actual:** The console shows: "A PressResponder was rendered without a pressable child. Either call the usePress hook, or wrap your DOM node with <Pressable> component." It comes from `_next/static/chunks/node_modules_next_dist_1e8vcs8._.js`, up to 11 times per visit on `/chip/settlements`.

This usually means a React Aria trigger (tooltip, disclosure or dialog trigger) wraps an element that does not forward press events, so that element may not respond to keyboard or press input. It is worth checking whether this is connected to BUG-002, but I have no direct evidence of a link.

**Evidence:** Signal `console-warning`, 13 deduplicated keys, 41 total occurrences.

**Found by:** admin-change-setting__webkit, admin-first-look__webkit, admin-money-check__webkit

## Usability issues

### Admin portal

1. **Staff-facing screens show developer jargon and internal details** (2 personas).
   - Examples: "Round 16" / "Round 22" on Email package prices; "meikigo-api" and "meikigo-brand" on Plan caps; "CHIP mock mode".
   - The Money banner tells business users to "Set `MEIKIGO_CHIP_MOCK=false` to use the real API". Row details show raw CHIP IDs such as `si_3120`.
   - Aina (first-look): *"Labels like 'meikigo-api,' 'meikigo-brand,' and 'CHIP mock mode' assume you already know the system's internal architecture."*

2. **The "Override date" testing clock sits on the main Platform settings page** (2 personas). Both non-technical personas saw it as a risky developer control that looks related to business time settings.
   - Aina: *"The 'Override date' testing clock control sitting right on the main Platform settings page felt like a risky, easy-to-misuse toggle for a business person to stumble across."*
   - Encik Rahman: *"it is clearly a developer/testing feature … so I left it alone too."*

3. **Money data does not match the Shop directory, and it is only clear late that it is sample data** (2 personas).
   - The Dashboard and Money screens list "Abu Barbershop", "Kedai Gunting Sri Muda" and "The Fade Room". The Shop directory contains only "Wak Jono Barber".
   - Users find out the data is fake only from a dim banner (see BUG-006).
   - Aina: *"I spent a few steps thinking data was missing before realizing the Money module is sample/mock data."*
   - Wei Ling: *"the whole answer I'm giving my boss is based on test data … I'll need to caveat my answer heavily."*

4. **The same thing has several names: complaints, chargebacks and disputes** (1 persona). The nav says "Payment complaints", the dashboard card says "Open chargebacks", and the URL is `/chip/disputes`.
   - Wei Ling: *"a bit much to track for someone not familiar with payment jargon."*

5. **"Net payout" vs "Gross/Withheld" wording, and an ambiguous "Payouts waiting" card** (1 persona). This is separate from the calculation bug in BUG-001.
   - The terms in the column and in the Details panel do not line up.
   - "Payouts waiting: 3" can be read as money already paid.
   - The "Sending" status is explained only in a tooltip, so the user could not tell whether to count it as settled.

6. **Settings are hard to scan: many sub-tabs and no search** (1 persona). Encik Rahman opened 11 settings pages one by one looking for "support hours". The setting does not exist, so giving up was correct, but a search box would have told him that in seconds.
   - *"The Settings section is split into many sub-tabs … which took a while to click through one by one since there was no search function within Settings."*
   - The only match, "Hours per support visit", is a duration and could be confused with opening hours.

7. **The greeting and profile card show the raw email address** (seen in all 3 sessions and my screenshot `_triage/repro-sidebar-first-click.png`).
   - The dashboard heading reads "Hi <email address>." instead of the user's name.
   - The expanded profile card prints the same email twice (as the name and again as the email).
   - This is cosmetic, but it looks unfinished.

## Not tested / limits

- **Only the admin app was in this run.** No merchant, POS or booking personas ran, so login, booking, selling and customer payment flows in those apps were not covered.
- **Money screens run on mock data** ("Sample data. Payment endpoints are not live yet … no money moves"). BUG-001 is proven on the mock data. Whether the real CHIP API path has the same net/gross error is not known.
- **The external CHIP "Receipt" link** (`https://gate.chip-in.asia/receipts/si_3120`) was not followed, because it leaves the Meikigo site. With a mock payout ID it will probably not open a real receipt.
- **The persona runs saved no screenshots** (every signal has `shots: []`, and there are no `evidence/` folders). Evidence comes from page snapshots, session logs and my own screenshots in `_triage/`.
- **Only WebKit** on a desktop-size screen. Chromium, Firefox and mobile layouts were not tested.
- **The "support hours" task could not test save-and-persist** because no such setting exists. Settings persistence is still untested (see Coverage).
- **Role coverage:** all personas used the same ADMIN account, which has the Finance role. Other staff roles and permission limits were not tested.
- **Reproduction scope:** I reproduced 4 items (BUG-001, BUG-002, BUG-003, BUG-004). BUG-006 was not reproduced.
