## Rules this follows (from meikigo-marketing-site/MESSAGING.md)

- Don't say "no sign-up": customers book without an app but do create an account in the browser.
- Don't promise "no fee" for DuitNow or card, and don't show tapping a card on the tablet.
- Don't claim offline mode, per-barber commission or payroll.
- Don't show shop counts or invented shop names as proof. "Demo Barbershop" in the footage is a demo, not proof.
- Fresha is cheaper for 1–2 barbers, so the price frame is for shops with 3 or more barbers.
  Re-check fresha.com/pricing before each send if more than a month has passed.
