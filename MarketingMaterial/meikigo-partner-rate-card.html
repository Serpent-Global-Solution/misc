How to quote

  • Quote GLCs and large corporates in the upper half of each range. They usually need more documentation, security reviews and UAT rounds.
  • Never quote below the low end of a range. The commission is already inside it.
  • Give a range only at first contact. Meikigo issues the firm quotation after a scoped discovery phase.
  • Fixed-price payment split: 30% on signing, 40% across milestones, 30% on UAT sign-off.
  • Every build includes a warranty period for defect fixes. The client owns the IP and source code at handover.
