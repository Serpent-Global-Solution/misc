## What Meikigo is

Meikigo is a barbershop operations platform for Malaysia: one tablet handles walk-in queue, bookings, and payment (cash, DuitNow, card), plus reports. Expanding later to retail/F&B — so public-facing copy should stay general enough not to box Meikigo in as barbershop-only forever, but today's proof and examples are barbershop-specific.

Brand name: **Meikigo** (never "Miki" in public-facing copy — that's an internal/dev nickname).
Tiers: **Starter / Plus / Pro** (never internal codenames).

## Pricing anchors (public-facing)

- Price floor: **From RM109/mo (Starter)**
- Trial: 14-day Plus-equivalent, no card, no contract
- Cost comparison: Starter RM109/mo vs Fresha Team RM135/mo (3 barbers × RM45/staff, excl. tax/add-ons, checked 27 Oct 2026). Per-staff pricing is cheaper for 1–2 barbers — say so honestly when relevant.

## Voice spine / positioning

- H1 style: "This is how a barbershop should run."
- Sub style: "Scan to book. No app. One tablet for queue, pay, and reports."
- Pain framing: "Stop juggling three different apps" — walk-ins, bookings, and the till, finally together.
- Compare stance: honest, not aggressive. Booking apps (Fresha etc.) stop at the appointment; Meikigo covers booking + payment + reporting on one tablet. Qualify prospects still running paper books, hand-counted commissions, or app-download friction.

## Hard "do not say" list (compliance-critical — these are wrong claims about the actual product)

- Don't claim "offline-first" / "works without internet" — not a real feature.
- Don't say "Trusted by 100+ barbershops" — unverified. Use capability claims (devices, tenders, BYOD) instead of shop counts until real pilots exist. Label testimonials as early notes, not social proof.
- Don't position payroll/payslips as a default feature — Pro-tier only.
- Don't claim marketplace/discovery features — don't have them.
- Don't say "your own DuitNow QR," "no fee," or "no take" on payments — DuitNow/card run through Meikigo's CHIP payment account with a processing fee.
- Don't describe NFC / tap-to-pay / card-tap on the tablet — customers pay by scanning a QR on their own phone, not tapping a device.
- Don't say "no sign-up" for customers — booking is no-app, but customers do create a browser account.
- Don't describe per-barber, flat, or tiered commission rates — commission is one service % + one product % per outlet.
- Don't use "~RM200/mo" for competitor per-staff pricing — use Fresha's public RM45/team member (RM135 for 3 barbers), with the check date cited.
- Don't invent shop names or fabricate testimonials.
- WhatsApp CRM is **not live** — current customer messaging is email-only. Don't promise WhatsApp-based communication in sales or marketing copy yet.

## Legal/compliance guardrail for sales & marketing copy

Any policy-sensitive claim (data handling, refunds, contracts) should default to what Malaysian law requires (PDPA 2010, Consumer Protection Act 1999, Contracts Act 1950) rather than a marketing-convenient claim. When in doubt, flag it rather than asserting it in outward-facing copy.
