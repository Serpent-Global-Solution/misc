# Meikigo Client Privacy Notice

**Version 1.0 — Draft, target effective date 31 October 2026 (pending legal review — see `legal/README.md`)**

*This version number and date are what is recorded against your account when you accept it.*

---

This notice explains how Serpents Capital Solution (trading as "Meikigo") ("Meikigo", "we", "us"), a partnership registered in Malaysia under the Registration of Businesses Act 1956 (registration no. 202603217394 (003880138-D)), collects, uses, and protects your personal data when you use the Meikigo customer app, in accordance with Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 ("PDPA"). It should be read alongside our [Terms of Service](./client-terms-of-service.md).

## 1. What personal data we collect

**Directly from you, at signup:** your full name, email address, phone number, and a password (or, if you sign up through Google, the name and email Google provides on your behalf).

**From your use of the app:** the services, staff, and outlets you browse or select; your queue tickets and bookings; your transaction history at the Merchants you visit (recorded by the Merchant at their point of sale, not directly by you in this app); your loyalty point and punch-card balances; your referral code and referral history; and any star ratings you submit.

**From a Merchant, if they add you without you signing up yourself:** if a barbershop staff member creates a record for you at the counter, or if a Merchant imports an existing customer list of theirs into Meikigo, your name, phone number, and (if provided) email and an opening loyalty balance may exist in our systems before you ever create your own account. This record is unverified and cannot be used to log in. When you register with a matching phone number, we show you which Merchants hold a record under that number and ask you to confirm the records are yours before linking them to your account. Records you do not confirm are not linked to you. This protects you, and the previous holder of a reissued phone number, from seeing someone else's history.

**On your device:** the app keeps your login session in your browser's storage so you stay signed in.

**What is required and what is optional:** your full name, email address, and phone number are required to create an account and to join a queue or make a booking. Without them we cannot provide the service. Marketing consent is optional, and refusing it does not affect your use of the app.

## 2. Why we collect it

We collect and use your personal data to: operate your account and let you log in; let you join a queue or make a booking and show that to the Merchant you're visiting; maintain your loyalty and referral balances; record the transaction history a Merchant needs for its own accounting; send you transactional communications (appointment reminders, receipts, verification codes); and, only with your separate marketing consent, send you promotional messages on a Merchant's behalf.

## 3. Marketing consent

Marketing consent is captured separately from your acceptance of our Terms of Service, as an unticked checkbox you must actively select. You can withdraw it at any time, and withdrawal takes effect immediately.

**If a Merchant imports your details from their own existing records** (rather than you signing up yourself), and the Merchant declares — as part of that import — that you had already agreed to receive marketing from them, we record that this consent came from the Merchant's own declaration, not directly from you. We keep a record of which kind of consent applies to your account (self-given, or merchant-declared) so this can always be traced. In that case, the first marketing email you receive from that Merchant will tell you that you are receiving it because the Merchant indicated you had previously agreed, and will include a one-click unsubscribe link. Either way, you can unsubscribe or withdraw consent at any time, and doing so is honoured immediately regardless of how the consent was originally recorded.

## 4. Who can see your data

**Merchants** can see your name, phone number, transaction history, and loyalty balance at their own outlets only — never at a different Merchant, and never your star ratings (see Section 6).

**Meikigo's own staff** cannot view your personal data without first recording a genuine business reason for doing so. This applies to every Meikigo staff member without exception, including the most senior staff. Every access is logged together with the reason given, and access logs are themselves periodically reviewed within Meikigo, by someone other than the staff whose access is being reviewed.

**Meikigo support staff can access a Merchant's account** — including customer records held under that Merchant — to help configure the Merchant's shop or resolve a support issue. Opening this kind of support access always requires a stated reason, is time-limited, and is logged. Where a Merchant is on our PRO subscription tier, any change made during such a session is shown on that Merchant's own audit log, so the Merchant can see what was done and when.

## 5. Service providers who process your data, and where

We use a small number of service providers to help run Meikigo. Each is only given the data it needs to do its job, is contractually required to protect it, and may not use it for its own purposes:

- **Supabase** — account authentication and the underlying database. Supabase's infrastructure for this project is located in **Singapore**, not Malaysia (see "Cross-border transfer" below).
- **CHIP** — processes the card and DuitNow payments you make at a Merchant's counter (see our [Terms of Service](./client-terms-of-service.md), Section 4). Meikigo does not see or store your card details — CHIP handles that directly.
- **Brevo** — sends transactional email (verification codes, receipts, booking reminders) and, only where you've given marketing consent, promotional email on a Merchant's behalf. Brevo processes your name and email address on servers in the **European Union** (see "Cross-border transfer" below).
- **Vercel Analytics** — used on our marketing website only (not the customer app itself), to measure which pages lead to signups. It does not use cookies and does not collect data that identifies you personally.

**Cross-border transfer**: your personal data is transferred outside Malaysia to be stored and processed — to Singapore (Supabase) and to the European Union (Brevo). We make these transfers in accordance with Section 129 of the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA), on the basis that each destination has in force a law substantially similar to the PDPA (Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012, and the EU General Data Protection Regulation), and that Meikigo maintains binding contractual commitments with each provider requiring your personal data to be protected to a level at least equivalent to that required under Malaysian law. Your consent at account creation is an additional basis for these transfers.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

## 6. Anonymity of reviews

If you submit a star rating for an outlet or a staff member, the Merchant and the staff member being rated cannot see who submitted it, and it is never shown alongside your name or account in any Merchant-facing or public view. Meikigo does not disclose who submitted a rating, except where required by law.

## 7. How long we keep your data

Transaction records (what you bought, from which Merchant, when, and for how much) are financial and tax records under Malaysian law and are generally retained for at least seven years, consistent with LHDN (Inland Revenue Board of Malaysia) record-keeping practice, regardless of what later happens to your account.

If you delete your account, we remove your ability to log in and hold a loyalty balance, and any unredeemed points or referral credit are forfeited. Your past transaction records are kept for the tax retention period described above, but your name, email address, and phone number are removed from them and replaced with an anonymous reference, so those records can no longer be linked to you. The one exception is an individual e-invoice issued in your name at your request: it must be kept as issued, with your details, for the period LHDN requires.

Marketing-related consent records and blast recipient history are retained for 12 months after account cancellation/deletion. Support communications are retained for 3 years.

## 8. Backups

We take a daily backup of our database, retained for 30 days, with point-in-time recovery enabled. **This describes what we do — it is not a guarantee against data loss.** We test our ability to restore from backup on a recurring basis as an internal operational practice.

## 9. If there is a data breach

If we become aware of a personal data breach affecting your data that is likely to cause you significant harm, we will notify you by email without undue delay, telling you what happened, what data was involved, what we are doing about it, and what you can do to protect yourself. We will also notify the Personal Data Protection Commissioner where the PDPA requires it.

## 10. Your rights under the PDPA

You have the right to:

- **Request access** to the personal data we hold about you, and to **request correction** of data that is inaccurate. Because of the retention position explained in Section 7, a request to access or correct your data may not extend to historical financial transaction records, which we are required to retain.
- **Request a copy of your personal data in a commonly used electronic format**, or ask us to send it to another organisation, where technically feasible (data portability).
- **Withdraw your consent** to our processing of your personal data, at any time. This is broader than marketing consent (Section 3) — if you withdraw consent entirely, we will stop processing your data for the purposes that depended on it, though this may mean we can no longer provide you with the parts of the service that require it, and it does not affect our right or obligation to retain data for another lawful reason (for example, the financial-record retention in Section 7).
- **Request that we stop, or not begin, processing your data where doing so is likely to cause you damage or distress**, under section 42 of the PDPA. We will consider this request and respond to you within the timeframe the PDPA requires.
- **Lodge a complaint** with Malaysia's Department of Personal Data Protection (Jabatan Perlindungan Data Peribadi), Level 8, Galeria PjH, Jalan P4W, Persiaran Perdana, Presint 4, Pusat Pentadbiran Kerajaan Persekutuan, 62100 Putrajaya — by phone at 03-7456 3888, by email at aduan@pdp.gov.my, or online at daftar.pdp.gov.my/p_aduan — if you believe we have not handled your personal data in accordance with the PDPA.

To exercise any of these rights, or with any question about this notice, contact our Data Protection Officer at support@meikigo.com (please put "PDPA request" in the subject line). We will respond to an access or correction request within 21 days of receiving it, as the PDPA requires. You can also write to us by post at F-0203, Emporis Duplex, Psn Surian, Jalan Teknologi, Sek 3, Kota Damansara, 47810 Petaling Jaya, Selangor, Malaysia.
