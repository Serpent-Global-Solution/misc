This notice explains how Meikigo [PLACEHOLDER: legal entity name] ("Meikigo", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your personal data when you use the Meikigo customer app, in accordance with Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 ("PDPA"). It should be read alongside our [Terms of Service](./client-terms-of-service.md).

## 1. What personal data we collect

**Directly from you, at signup:** your full name, email address, phone number, and a password (or, if you sign up through Google, the name and email Google provides on your behalf).

**From your use of the app:** the services, staff, and outlets you browse or select; your queue tickets and bookings; your transaction history at the Merchants you visit (recorded by the Merchant at their point of sale, not directly by you in this app); your loyalty point and punch-card balances; your referral code and referral history; and any star ratings you submit.

**From a Merchant, if they add you without you signing up yourself:** if a barbershop staff member creates a record for you at the counter, or if a Merchant imports an existing customer list of theirs into Meikigo, your name, phone number, and (if provided) email and an opening loyalty balance may exist in our systems before you ever create your own account. This record is unverified and cannot be used to log in until you complete registration yourself with a matching phone number, at which point it becomes your account.

## 2. Why we collect it

We collect and use your personal data to: operate your account and let you log in; let you join a queue or make a booking and show that to the Merchant you're visiting; maintain your loyalty and referral balances; record the transaction history a Merchant needs for its own accounting; send you transactional communications (appointment reminders, receipts, verification codes); and, only with your separate marketing consent, send you promotional messages on a Merchant's behalf.

## 3. Marketing consent

Marketing consent is captured separately from your acceptance of our Terms of Service, as an unticked checkbox you must actively select. You can withdraw it at any time, and withdrawal takes effect immediately.

**If a Merchant imports your details from their own existing records** (rather than you signing up yourself), and the Merchant declares — as part of that import — that you had already agreed to receive marketing from them, we record that this consent came from the Merchant's own declaration, not directly from you. We keep a record of which kind of consent applies to your account (self-given, or merchant-declared) so this can always be traced. Either way, you can unsubscribe or withdraw consent at any time, and doing so is honoured immediately regardless of how the consent was originally recorded.

## 4. Who can see your data

**Merchants** can see your name, phone number, transaction history, and loyalty balance at their own outlets only — never at a different Merchant, and never your star ratings (see Section 6).

**Meikigo's own staff** cannot view your personal data without first recording a genuine business reason for doing so. This applies to every Meikigo staff member without exception, including the most senior staff. Every access is logged together with the reason given, and access logs are themselves periodically reviewed within Meikigo, by someone other than the staff whose access is being reviewed.

**Meikigo support staff can access a Merchant's account** — including customer records held under that Merchant — to help configure the Merchant's shop or resolve a support issue. Opening this kind of support access always requires a stated reason, is time-limited, and is logged. Where a Merchant is on our PRO subscription tier, any change made during such a session is shown on that Merchant's own audit log, so the Merchant can see what was done and when.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

## 5. Anonymity of reviews

If you submit a star rating for an outlet or a staff member, it is anonymous in both directions: the Merchant and the staff member being rated cannot see who submitted it, and it is never shown alongside your name or account in any Merchant-facing or public view.

## 6. How long we keep your data

Transaction records (what you bought, from which Merchant, when, and for how much) are financial and tax records under Malaysian law and are generally retained for at least seven years, consistent with LHDN (Inland Revenue Board of Malaysia) record-keeping practice, regardless of what later happens to your account.

If you delete your account, we remove your ability to log in and hold a loyalty balance, and any unredeemed points or referral credit are forfeited. **We do not promise that all your data is erased when you delete your account** — your name, email, and phone number remain attached to your past transaction records, because those are retained as financial records as described above. We are telling you this plainly rather than promising erasure we cannot actually deliver.

## 7. Backups

We take a daily backup of our database, retained for 30 days, with point-in-time recovery enabled. **This describes what we do — it is not a guarantee against data loss.** We test our ability to restore from backup on a recurring basis as an internal operational practice.

## 8. Your rights under the PDPA

You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, and to request that inaccurate data be corrected. Because of the retention position explained in Section 6, a request to access or correct your data may not extend to historical financial transaction records, which we are required to retain.
