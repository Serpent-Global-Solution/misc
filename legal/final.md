# Part 1 — Client Terms of Service

*For customers using the Meikigo app to browse, queue, and book appointments.*

### 1. Who this agreement is with
These terms are an agreement between you (the customer) and **Serpents Capital Solution** ("Meikigo", "we", "us").  
Meikigo provides the technology platform (currently a mobile web app) that lets you browse service shops, check live queues, and book appointments. Each shop ("Merchant") is an independent business that provides the actual haircut or grooming service. Meikigo is the technology connecting you with the shop — we are not the barbershop or salon itself.

### 2. Who can use the app
* **Age requirement:** You must be at least 18 years old to create your own account. We do not provide separate accounts for minors. If you are bringing a child for a service (such as a haircut), you can book it through your own account as an extra service line.
* **One account per phone number:** Each mobile phone number can only be linked to one Meikigo account. You cannot create a second account using an existing phone number.

### 3. Creating and protecting your account
* You can sign up using your full name, email address, phone number, and a password, or sign in through Google.
* We verify your email address by sending you a one-time security code (we do not use SMS verification codes).
* If you sign up using Google, you will need to add and confirm your phone number before you can join a queue or confirm a booking.
* Please keep your login details safe. You are responsible for all bookings and activity under your account.

### 4. What the app does (and what it doesn't do)
* **What you can do:** Browse shop details, view live queue positions, view barbers and staff, check service prices, join walk-in queues, book appointment times, check your ticket status, and leave star ratings after your visit.
* **Counter payments only:** The Meikigo customer app does not charge your card or take payments. You pay for your haircut or grooming products directly at the shop counter.
* **No push notifications or SMS alerts:** The app does not send text messages or push notifications about queue changes. You are responsible for checking your ticket status in the app. The only messages we send are:
  1. Important service emails (like appointment reminders, one-time verification codes, and digital receipts).
  2. In-app notices if a shop cancels your booking or temporarily closes (which will always include a reason).

### 5. Queueing and booking
* **Choosing your barber:** When you join a queue or book an appointment, you must pick the staff member you want (there is no "any staff" option).
* **Guaranteed service prices:** The price shown when you join the queue or book is the price you pay at the counter, even if the shop updates its pricing before your visit. If you add extra services at the counter after arriving, those extras are charged at the shop's current price. If the shop adds a surcharge for paying by card or DuitNow QR, it will be shown to you before you pay.
* **Queue timing:** A booking reserves a time slot, but slight delays can happen if a previous customer's haircut takes longer than expected. A walk-in queue ticket indicates your order in line, not an exact minute on the clock.
* **Cancellations and no-shows:**
  * You can cancel your booking in the app at any time before your appointment without any fee or penalty.
  * If you do not show up and did not cancel, the shop may mark your appointment as a "no-show." A shop can only deduct loyalty points for a no-show if its no-show policy was shown to you in the app before you booked, and the deduction can never be more than the points that visit would have earned you.
* **If the shop cancels on you:** If a shop cancels your confirmed booking or cannot serve you, no penalty applies to you. The shop will offer you priority rebooking for their next available slot, and they may also give you goodwill loyalty points at their discretion.

### 6. Loyalty points and referral rewards
* Shops can set up their own loyalty programs (points, digital stamp cards, or referral rewards) and choose their own rules, reward values, and expiry dates.
* Loyalty points belong to each individual shop and cannot be transferred to a different shop.
* **Permanent loss upon account deletion:** If you choose to delete your Meikigo account, all your unspent loyalty points and referral rewards are permanently wiped. Before you confirm deletion, the app will show you your remaining balances across all shops and ask you to confirm that you understand they will be permanently lost. Once deleted, lost points cannot be restored.

### 7. Star ratings and reviews
* After a completed visit, you can leave an anonymous 1 to 5 star rating for the shop and for the staff member who served you.
* Ratings are anonymous: the shop and staff cannot see who left a rating, and we do not share your identity except where required by law.
* There is no text review box. Once submitted, a star rating cannot be edited, deleted, or removed (even if your service is later refunded).

### 8. Marketing emails
* Shops may only send you promotional marketing emails if you check an optional, separate marketing consent box during signup. This box is never pre-checked.
* You can withdraw your marketing consent at any time in your account settings or by clicking unsubscribe in any promotional email.
* Essential transactional messages (receipts, booking reminders, security codes) are not marketing emails and will always be sent so you can use the service.

### 9. What records we keep
Malaysian tax and accounting rules (Inland Revenue Board / LHDN) require businesses to keep sales and financial transaction records for at least 7 years. Because of this legal requirement, your past sales receipts are kept if you delete your account, but with your name, email, and phone number removed (see Section 10). We explain our full retention schedule clearly in our [Privacy Notice](#part-2--client-privacy-notice).

### 10. Deleting your account
You can delete your account at any time inside the app. Deleting your account removes your login, closes your active profile, and clears your loyalty points as described in Section 6. Past financial receipts are kept for statutory tax compliance, but your name, email, and phone number are removed from them and replaced with an anonymous reference. The only exception is an individual e-invoice issued in your name at your request, which must be kept as issued.

Please use Meikigo responsibly and treat others with respect. You agree not to:

### 12. Account suspension

# Part 2 — Client Privacy Notice

*How we handle your personal data under Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA).*

This notice explains how **Serpents Capital Solution** ("Meikigo", "we", "us") collects, uses, and safeguards your personal data when you use the Meikigo customer app.

### 1. What personal data we collect
* **Information you give us directly:** Your full name, email address, mobile phone number, and password (or the profile name and email provided if you sign in with Google).
* **Information generated when you use the app:** The shops, barbers, and services you view; your bookings and queue tickets; your visit and counter purchase history; your loyalty point balances; your referral activity; and any star ratings you submit.
* **Information created by a shop at the counter:** If a shop creates a customer profile for you at their counter before you download the app, or if they import past customer records into Meikigo, your name, phone number, and past shop visits may already be in our system. When you register with that same phone number, we show you which shops hold a record under it and ask you to confirm they are yours before linking them. Records you don't confirm are not linked to you, which protects you and anyone who previously had a reissued phone number.
* **On your device:** The app keeps your login session in your browser's storage so you stay signed in.
* **Required vs optional:** Your name, email address, and phone number are required to create an account and to queue or book — without them we cannot provide the service. Marketing consent is optional, and saying no does not affect your use of the app.

### 2. Why we collect your information
We collect and use your data to:
* Set up your account and let you sign in securely.
* Help you join queues, book appointments, and show your bookings to the shop.
* Keep track of your loyalty points, punch cards, and referral bonuses.
* Record sales and counter receipts for legal accounting and tax purposes.
* Send you essential service messages (verification codes, receipts, booking reminders).
* Send you promotional marketing emails (only if you gave separate consent).

### 3. How marketing consent works
* Marketing consent is strictly optional. It is requested through an unticked checkbox at signup and is never bundled with these terms. You can withdraw your consent at any time with immediate effect.
* **If a shop imported your contact details:** If a shop imported your contact info from their old records and declared that you previously agreed to receive marketing, we record that this consent came from the shop's declaration. The first marketing email you get from that shop will tell you why you are receiving it and include a one-click unsubscribe link. You can still opt out or unsubscribe at any time, and your request will be honoured immediately.

### 4. Who can see your data
* **Shops:** A shop can only see your name, phone number, visit history, and loyalty balance for their own outlets. They can never see your visits to other shops, and they can never see who gave them a star rating.
* **Meikigo staff:** Our staff cannot look at your personal data without entering a valid, documented business reason first. Every single access is recorded in an audit log, and the logs are reviewed by someone other than the person who made the access.
* **Meikigo technical support:** When a shop asks our support team for technical help, support staff may temporarily view shop records. If the shop is on our PRO subscription, every action taken by Meikigo support is clearly displayed on the shop owner's own audit log.

### 5. Trusted service providers and overseas data storage
We use a small number of reputable technology providers to help run Meikigo. Each provider is given only the minimum data needed to perform its job, is under strict confidentiality obligations, and cannot use your data for its own purposes:
* **Supabase (Database and logins):** Our core database and authentication servers are hosted with Supabase in **Singapore**.
* **CHIP (Payment processing):** CHIP processes all card and DuitNow QR payments at shop counters. Meikigo never sees, collects, or stores your credit or debit card numbers.
* **Brevo (Email service):** Delivers verification codes, booking reminders, receipts, and approved marketing emails. Brevo processes your name and email address on servers in the **European Union**.
* **Vercel Analytics:** Measures visits to our public marketing website only. It is completely cookieless and does not track personal customer data.

**Overseas transfers (PDPA Section 129):** Your data is transferred outside Malaysia to Singapore (Supabase) and the European Union (Brevo). We rely on the fact that both places have data protection laws substantially similar to Malaysia's (Singapore's PDPA 2012 and the EU's GDPR), backed by binding contracts with each provider requiring protection at least equal to Malaysian law. Your consent at signup is an additional basis.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party.

### 6. Star ratings are anonymous
When you rate a shop or a barber, the shop and barber will see their updated average rating, but they will never see your name or account attached to it. We do not disclose who left a rating, except where required by law.

### 7. How long we keep your data
* **Sales and tax records:** Kept for **at least 7 years**, in line with Malaysian Inland Revenue Board (LHDN) statutory requirements.
* **Marketing consent records:** Kept for **12 months** after account deletion or cancellation.
* **Customer support tickets:** Kept for **3 years**.
* **Account deletion and data erasure:** If you delete your account, your login is closed and your loyalty points are cleared. Your past sales receipts are kept as financial records under Malaysian law, but your name, email, and phone number are removed from them and replaced with an anonymous reference, so they can no longer be linked to you. The only exception is an individual e-invoice issued in your name at your request, which LHDN requires us to keep as issued.

### 8. Daily backups
We run automated daily backups of our database, kept for 30 days with point-in-time recovery. This describes our operational safeguard and standard practice — it is not a legal guarantee against unexpected data loss.

### 9. If there is a data breach
If we discover a data breach affecting your personal data that is likely to cause you significant harm, we will email you without undue delay, explaining what happened, what data was involved, what we are doing about it, and what you can do to protect yourself. We will also notify the Personal Data Protection Commissioner where the PDPA requires it.

### 10. Your rights under Malaysia's PDPA
Under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010, you have the right to:
1. **Access and correct:** Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you and ask us to fix any mistakes (this does not include altering historical tax receipts).
2. **Take your data with you:** Request a copy of your personal data in a commonly used electronic format, or ask us to send it to another organisation, where technically feasible.
3. **Withdraw consent:** Withdraw your permission for us to process your data at any time.
4. **Prevent distress:** Ask us to stop or limit processing your data if it is likely to cause you unjustified damage or distress (under Section 42 of the PDPA).
5. **Lodge a complaint:** File a complaint with Malaysia's Department of Personal Data Protection (JPDP):
   * **Address:** Level 8, Galeria PjH, Jalan P4W, Persiaran Perdana, Presint 4, 62100 Putrajaya.
   * **Phone:** 03-7456 3888
   * **Email:** `aduan@pdp.gov.my`
   * **Online:** `daftar.pdp.gov.my/p_aduan`

To exercise any of these rights, email our Data Protection Officer at `support@meikigo.com` with "PDPA request" in the subject line. We will respond to access and correction requests within 21 days, as the PDPA requires.

* **Surcharge display (Client ToS §5, Merchant Terms §4):** If a shop passes the card/QR fee on, the POS must show the surcharge to the customer before payment.
* **Merchant data retention (Merchant Terms §3):** A scheduled job must delete or anonymise personal data of Brands that have been closed or read-only for 24 months, keeping tax-required transaction records.
* **Pseudonymisation on account deletion (Client ToS §10, Privacy Notice §7):** Account deletion (not built yet) must strip name, email, and phone from past transactions and replace them with an anonymous reference, except individual e-invoices issued in the customer's name.
* **Confirm-before-linking (Privacy Notice §1):** At registration, show the shops holding a record under the phone number and link only the records the customer confirms.
* **Data portability (Privacy Notice §10):** A way to give a customer their personal data in a common electronic format on request (a manual export by the DPO is enough to start).

### 4. Decisions taken under Malaysian law (2026-10-31)
Each open policy question was resolved by following what Malaysian law requires:
* **Retention after a Brand stops using Meikigo (PDPA s.10, retention principle):** fixed 24-month period, then deletion/anonymisation except tax records; export available throughout.
* **Customer identity on retained transactions (PDPA s.10):** tax retention covers the transaction, not the customer's identity, so identity is pseudonymised on account deletion. Exception: individual e-invoices issued in the customer's name at their request, kept as issued per LHDN e-invoicing rules.
* **Records linked by phone number (PDPA s.9, security principle):** confirmation step before linking, to prevent disclosure to the holder of a reissued number.
* **Card/QR surcharging:** confirmed permitted by CHIP; the Merchant must display it before payment.
* **Bahasa Malaysia (PDPA s.7(3)):** a BM Privacy Notice is drafted (`notis-privasi-pelanggan.md`) for review alongside the English text. Counsel or a certified translator should confirm the BM wording.
* **Contracting party:** no change. A registered partnership may contract and be sued in its firm name.
* **Data portability and device storage:** portability added to the rights list; login-session storage disclosed. Counsel to confirm the commencement status of the portability right.
* **Force majeure:** not legally required; left to counsel's discretion.
